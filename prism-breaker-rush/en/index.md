## 1. Scope

This policy applies to the Prism Breaker Rush web version and Android/game-portal editions of the same game. Availability and features can differ by distribution channel. When using Poki, CrazyGames or Google Play, also review the relevant platform's privacy policy. Describing a distribution channel here does not mean that the game has been released or approved on that channel.

## 2. Game progress and device storage

No account is required. The game stores your best score, highest cleared floor, coins, continue totals, cleared-board records, seen tips, and sound/language preferences in device web storage (localStorage). Android editions use the app's own web storage; web editions use storage in the relevant browser. Diagnostic records used to investigate errors may also be stored locally.

The game does not ask you to enter your name, email address, real name or contact details, and does not automatically upload this local progress, settings or diagnostic data to a separate server operated by us. Platform SDK processing and web access information are explained separately below.

Coins are in-game currency. The current game has no cash redemption or in-app purchases.

## 3. Web hosting, platform SDKs and advertising

- **Directly hosted web edition:** Cloudflare Pages delivers the game files. Cloudflare may process access information such as IP addresses and request details for delivery and security. The directly hosted browser edition does not include advertising or analytics SDKs separately inserted by the game.
- **Poki edition:** The Poki SDK receives signals such as loading completion and gameplay start/stop. Platform commercial and rewarded advertisements may be used; rewarded continues are your choice. Poki and advertising providers may process cookies, device/browser information and identifiers under their own policies to operate their platform and advertising services.
- **CrazyGames edition:** The CrazyGames SDK receives loading and gameplay start/stop signals. CrazyGames rewarded advertising is not enabled in the current game adapter. The platform's own service and advertising processing is governed by CrazyGames' policy.
- **Android / Google Play edition:** The current Android project uses Capacitor and includes the game files in the app. It does not include the Google AdMob advertising SDK or a separate analytics SDK. Game files run on the device; external links such as this policy require an internet connection. Google's policy governs Google Play distribution and store services.

If channel-specific features or advertising/analytics processing change, we update this policy and required store disclosures before the affected edition is released.

## 4. Device features and external links

Sound and haptics support gameplay effects. The current Android configuration uses Internet and vibration permissions; the game does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The privacy link in Settings opens through a browser or the platform's external-link feature. External sites are governed by their providers' policies.

## 5. Purposes and your choices

Local storage maintains game records, currency and preferences. You can change sound and language in Settings. Clearing the relevant browser site data or Android app data deletes local records and preferences; uninstalling the app also deletes its app-specific storage. Progress may not persist when device storage is unavailable.

We cannot recover or remotely delete device records that we do not separately hold. You can play without choosing rewarded advertising.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Device data remains until you clear the relevant site/app data or uninstall the app. We do not store the local progress, settings or diagnostic records described above on a separate server. Retention and deletion of information processed by platform/hosting providers follow their policies.

Contact details and inquiry content you provide by email are used to handle your inquiry. Contact emails are deleted within 30 days after the inquiry is resolved, unless the law requires longer retention.

## 8. Your rights and children

You may exercise rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction and withdrawal of consent under applicable law using the contact email above. We respond within the statutory period. In Korea, you may also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee.

The game has no account-registration feature that asks children to enter names, contact details or profiles. Check the relevant app store or portal for its age requirements and child/advertising protection settings.

## 9. Safeguards and changes

Game progress and preferences use device storage and are not automatically uploaded to a separate server operated by us. We update this policy and required store disclosures when features or processing change, and notify important changes as required by law.
